A CMWIM initiative

Canadian Muslim Women in Surgery

A pioneering support framework designed to empower Muslim women pursuing surgical careers in Canada.

CMWiS connects applicants with female residents in surgical subspecialties throughout Ontario, offering mentorship and guidance through the CaRMS application process — the point at which many students most need someone who has recently been through it.

While the initiative is focused on Muslim women, it also welcomes students from other marginalized communities who could benefit from mentorship.

The work rests on three things: representation, guidance, and empowerment — so that aspiring women surgeons can see a path and have someone walking it with them.
