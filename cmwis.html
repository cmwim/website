Canadian Muslim Women in Surgery

A pioneering support framework designed to empower Muslim women pursuing surgical careers in Canada.

Watch

Learn more about CMWiS

CMWiS connects applicants with female residents in surgical subspecialties throughout Ontario, offering mentorship and guidance through the CaRMS application process.

While the initiative focuses on Muslim women, it also welcomes students from other marginalized communities who could benefit from mentorship.

The work rests on representation, guidance and empowerment, so that aspiring women surgeons can see a path and have someone walking it with them.
