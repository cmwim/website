Past events

May 22, 2026 · 5:00–7:00 PM · ISNA Education Center, 2200 South Sheridan Way, Mississauga

Muslim Women in Medicine keynote with Dr. Aliya A. Khan

Internationally recognized physician, educator, researcher, and humanitarian Dr. Khan shared reflections on Muslim mentorship, female representation in healthcare, faith and medicine, leadership, resilience, identity, and building a meaningful career in healthcare.

May 22, 2026 · 5:00–7:00 PM · ISNA Education Center, 2200 South Sheridan Way, Mississauga

Muslim Women in Medicine Mentorship Networking Evening

Presented by CMWIM, the TMU, McMaster, and University of Toronto MMAC chapters, and ISNA Cares. Muslim women students connected with physician mentors across specialties in a relaxed setting designed to encourage honest questions, specialty exploration, shared mentorship, and meaningful community.

Conversation

Self-Doubt with Dr. Sondos Zayed

An honest conversation with Dr. Sondos Zayed about recognizing self-doubt, navigating it in medical training and practice, and moving forward with confidence and self-compassion.

January 14, 2026 · Campus initiative

Hijab Mask Extenders in Anatomy Labs

A practical accessibility initiative supporting hijab-wearing students with mask extenders in anatomy labs, helping personal protective equipment fit securely and comfortably.

MMAC Western · Physician panel

Giving Back Through Medicine

Dr. Ruba Kiwan and Dr. Nehal Al Tarhuni spoke with students about using their positions in medicine to advocate for and give back to their local communities.
