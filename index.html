Who we are

A community that understands your journey

The CMWIM Network brings together Muslim women physicians and medical students from across Canada to empower one another through a national mentorship program rooted in community, collaboration, and growth.

We pair members with mentors who understand the questions that don't always get asked out loud — about faith and practice, identity in clinical spaces, family, and the shape of a career that holds all of it.

Our promise

You should never have to navigate this alone.

Whether you're writing your first personal statement, choosing a specialty, or figuring out how to ask for prayer space on a surgical rotation — someone in this network has been there, and is happy to talk.

Mentorship program

Two ways to take part

Sign-ups are open year-round. Pairings are made with care — by chapter, specialty interest, and what you're hoping to get out of it.

Become a mentee

For medical students and trainees looking for guidance from someone who understands both the medicine and the rest of it. You'll be matched with a physician or senior resident in your region.

Mentee sign-up

Become a mentor

For physicians and residents. Mentors are paired with one or two mentees and typically connect two to three times a year, virtually or in person — the level of involvement is entirely up to you.

Upcoming events

What's coming up

Dates are announced on Instagram and in the WhatsApp community.

Specialty panels

Physicians and residents share what their specialty is actually like — the training, the hours, the parts nobody warns you about.

Faith & medicine conversations

Open discussions on practising medicine as a Muslim woman — from hijab in the OR to fasting on call to holding onto your values in clinical spaces.

Physician spotlights

Longer-form conversations with Muslim women physicians about how they built their careers, and what they'd do differently.
