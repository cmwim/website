National mentorship

Find guidance. Share what you know.

A Canada-wide community connecting Muslim women medical students with physicians who understand the questions, responsibilities, and possibilities that shape a life in medicine.

Mentorship is more than career advice. It is seeing that you belong here.

CMWIM creates space for honest conversations about medical training, specialties, faith, identity, leadership, and building a meaningful career.

Choose the path that fits where you are today.

Two ways to take part

Become a mentee

For medical students looking for guidance, shared perspective, and a stronger community in medicine.

  • Explore specialties and career paths
  • Ask questions in a supportive setting
  • Connect with mentors who share your values

Become a mentor

  • Support students navigating training
