National mentorship

Find guidance. Share what you know.

A Canada-wide community connecting Muslim women medical students with physicians who understand the questions, responsibilities, and possibilities that shape a life in medicine.

Mentorship is more than career advice. It is seeing that you belong here.

CMWIM creates space for honest conversations about medical training, specialties, faith, identity, leadership, and building a meaningful career.

Become a mentee

For Muslim women medical students who want candid guidance from a physician who understands medicine alongside faith, identity, and community.

  • Talk through specialty choices and training transitions
  • Learn from a physician’s lived experience
  • Build an ongoing connection beyond one-off advice

Become a mentor

For Muslim women physicians who want to make medical training less isolating by sharing the practical lessons they wish they had received earlier.

  • Be paired with one or two medical students
  • Connect virtually or in person as your schedule allows
  • Choose a schedule and level of involvement that work for you

Outreach toolkit

Mentee-to-mentor message template

Use this as a starting point when inviting a physician to join the CMWIM Mentorship Community. Personalize the highlighted fields before sending.

Salam Dr. [Mentor Name],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is [Mentee Name], and I’m a [year]-year medical student at [medical school]. I am currently serving as a school lead for the Canadian Muslim Women in Medicine (CMWIM) Mentorship Community.

Our initiative connects Muslim women physicians and trainees across Canada to foster mentorship, professional growth, and community. Each mentor is paired with one or two mentees, typically medical students, with full flexibility on meeting format and frequency. Most mentors connect with their mentees two to three times per year, virtually or in person, though the level of involvement is entirely up to you. We are looking to launch this mentorship initiative in January, inshaAllah.

We would be honoured to have you join our community as a mentor. Your presence would be valuable to members navigating their early career paths and identity within medicine.

If you’re interested, I’ve included our mentor sign-up form below. If you have any questions, I’d be happy to have a quick chat and tell you more about the initiative. Thank you for your time and consideration.
