Mentorship program

Mentorship

Our initiative connects Muslim women physicians and trainees across Canada to foster mentorship, professional growth, and community.

1. Sign up

Fill in a short form telling us where you train, what you're interested in, and what you're hoping for.

2. Get matched

Your chapter leads pair you thoughtfully — by region, specialty interest, and what you said you needed.

3. Connect

Meet at whatever cadence works for you both — virtually or in person. Most pairs connect a few times a year.

For students & trainees

Become a mentee

If you're a medical student or trainee looking for guidance from someone who understands both the medicine and everything around it, this is for you.

You'll be matched with a physician or senior resident — someone who can talk through specialty choice, CaRMS, research, and the questions that are harder to ask elsewhere: how to hold onto your practice through residency, how to navigate a clinical space where you're the only one who looks like you.

There's no minimum commitment. Take what's useful.

Mentee sign-up

Sign-ups are open year-round. Reach out and your chapter leads will get you matched.

For physicians & residents

Become a mentor

Mentors are paired with one or two mentees, typically medical students, with flexible meeting arrangements.

Most mentors connect with their mentees two to three times per year, virtually or in person — though the level of involvement is entirely up to you.

We would be honoured to have you join our network as a mentor. Your presence would be valuable to our members navigating their early career paths and identity within medicine.

Heading into a surgical specialty?
